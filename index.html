How to read this atlas

A starting point for research

This atlas contains two sets of seven fictional adult personas. It illustrates possible needs and viewpoints; it is not a customer survey, diagnosis, prediction model, or prescribing tool.

Four dimensions, kept separate

Age and gender describe the example person. Political identity is a fictional, self-described category. Treatment outlook describes their approach to care. No causal link between these dimensions is implied, and the categories are not exhaustive.

Reading the diagrams

  • The Venn diagram groups people by their stated priorities. The center means all three priorities, not a more valuable audience or a better medical candidate.
  • The radial diagram uses one equal slice per persona. Rings hold categories, not numeric scores. Filtering fades unmatched examples without changing slice sizes.
  • Goals and interview questions are hypotheses to explore with actual research participants.

Clinical context

Naltrexone is a prescription treatment used for alcohol use disorder and can reduce the urge to drink. Varenicline is a prescription medicine that helps adults quit smoking cigarettes. A clinician determines suitability and a treatment plan for each person.
